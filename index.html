Harvest Standoff

Game

Animals against farmhands, 5 a side. Nobody gets hurt, just very messy.

  • Plant itCarry the Stink-Pumpkin to A or B and hold E. If it goes off, your team wins the round.
  • Cork itDefenders win by corking the pumpkin, or by keeping it from being planted in time.
  • Shop smartWin rounds and splat opponents to earn corn coins. Teams swap jobs after 5 rounds. First to 6 wins.
